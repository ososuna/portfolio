# Portfolio

The personal portfolio of Oswaldo Osuna: one page, published in every Locale, presenting who he is, the Roles he has held, the Projects he has built and the Achievements he has earned.

## Language

**Locale**:
A language the whole portfolio is published in. `en` is the Default Locale; `es` is the other.
_Avoid_: Language, lang, translation

**Default Locale**:
The Locale served at the site root, without a Locale prefix in its URL.

**Section**:
One of the ordered parts of the page: Hero, Experience, Projects, Achievements, About.
_Avoid_: Block, panel

### Hero

**Hero**:
The opening Section: name, photo, Summary and Social Links.
_Avoid_: Main, home, header, intro

**Summary**:
The two-line statement in the Hero of who Oswaldo is and what he builds.
_Avoid_: Tagline, headline

**Social Link**:
A link to one of Oswaldo's profiles on an external network (GitHub, LinkedIn). The same in every Locale.
_Avoid_: Social media, social button

### Experience

**Experience**:
The Section listing every Role, newest first.
_Avoid_: Professional experience (as a term; it remains the Section's heading), work history

**Role**:
A position Oswaldo held at one Company over one Period, with the bullet points describing what he did there.
_Avoid_: Experience (for a single entry), job, position

**Company**:
An organization Oswaldo held a Role at, shown with its logo.
_Avoid_: Employer, client

**Period**:
The span of time a Role covered, written per Locale exactly as it should read (for example "October 2025 - Present").
_Avoid_: Date, duration

### Projects

**Project**:
Something Oswaldo built and presents with a title, description, image, Technologies and Project Links.
_Avoid_: Work, app, repo

**Project Link**:
A link from a Project to one of its destinations, identified by its kind: the live site, its source code, its UI source or its REST API source.
_Avoid_: Button, URL

**Technology**:
A language, framework, platform or tool a Project is built with, shown with its name and icon.
_Avoid_: Tag, skill, stack item

### Achievements

**Achievement**:
A recognition or credential Oswaldo has earned: an award, a program selection or a degree.
_Avoid_: Award, education, certification

### About

**About**:
The closing Section: a photo and the Bio Paragraphs.
_Avoid_: About me (as a term; it remains the Section's heading), bio section

**Bio Paragraph**:
One paragraph of Oswaldo's first-person introduction in the About Section.
_Avoid_: Description, bio fragment

**Highlight**:
A phrase within a Bio Paragraph shown emphasized.
_Avoid_: Bold, keyword
